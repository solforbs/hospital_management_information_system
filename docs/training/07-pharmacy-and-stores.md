# Pharmacy, stores and purchasing

For pharmacists, pharmacy technicians, storekeepers and procurement officers.

## Dispensing

1. Open **Pharmacy → Dispensing**. Prescriptions sent by clinicians appear in the queue.
2. Select a prescription and check the **five rights**: right patient, medicine, dose, route and time. Check the patient's **allergies**, shown in the banner.
3. If something is unclear or unsafe, **contact the prescriber** before dispensing. Do not guess.
4. **Dispense.** HMIS deducts the quantity from stock, and adds the medicine to the patient's bill or claim.
5. Counsel the patient on how to take the medicine.

Use **Cancel** for items that will not be dispensed (for example, out of stock or stopped by the prescriber), so the bill stays correct.

## Medicines and stock

- **Pharmacy → Medicines & stock** lists each medicine with its batches, quantities and **expiry dates**.
- Stock is issued from the batch that **expires first**. Expired batches are not dispensed.
- Record goods received against the correct batch number and expiry date, which make recalls and audits possible.
- Watch the low-stock and near-expiry indicators, and reorder in time.

## Stores and purchasing

**Stores & purchasing** manages non-pharmacy supplies and procurement:

1. Keep a list of approved **suppliers**.
2. Raise a **purchase order** with items, quantities and prices, and get it approved.
3. When goods arrive, **receive** them against the purchase order (goods received note). Only what was actually delivered goes into stock.
4. **Issue** stock to departments. Every movement is recorded, so the balance can be audited.

Never adjust stock to "make the numbers fit". Record the real reason (damage, expiry, count variance) so the audit trail stays honest.
