# Inpatient care, maternity and specialist services

For ward nurses, admission officers, ward clerks, midwives, theatre teams, and the clinicians who work on the wards.

## Admission

1. The clinician chooses **Request admission** in the consultation, with the ward and the reason.
2. The request appears under **Inpatient → Admissions**. The admissions office **allocates a free bed** (only clean, available beds are offered).
3. The patient's visit becomes *Admitted* and they appear on the **Ward board**.

## On the ward: the admission record

Open the patient from the ward board to reach their admission record. Use it to:

- **Record vitals** on the observation chart, at the times you take them.
- **Chart medication.** Each dose given (or not given, with the reason) is recorded against the prescription.
- **Add nursing and progress notes.**
- **Transfer** the patient to another bed or ward. The bed history keeps every move, and each ward's daily rate is billed for the days spent there.

Beds go through **available → occupied → cleaning → available**. Set the bed status on the ward board when cleaning is finished.

## Discharge

1. The clinician writes the **discharge summary**: diagnosis, treatment, medicines to take home and follow-up.
2. **Discharge** the patient from the admission record. HMIS bills the bed-days and frees the bed for cleaning.
3. If your hospital requires it, a cash patient with an **outstanding balance** cannot be discharged until it is settled at the cashier.
4. **Print** the discharge summary for the patient. It also supports SHA claims.

## Theatre and critical care

- **Theatre:** book the procedure (room, surgeon, date). On the day, complete the **safety checklist** before starting, then **start** and **complete** the procedure with its notes.
- **Critical care:** the ICU/HDU chart records frequent observations and the **fluid balance** (intake and output) for each admitted critical patient.

## Maternity, newborns and child welfare

- **ANC/PNC clinic:** register each pregnancy, record antenatal visits (MOH 405) and postnatal visits (MOH 406).
- **Labour ward:** fill in the **partograph** as labour progresses. HMIS shows alerts when progress crosses the **alert** and **action** lines. Then record the **delivery** (MOH 333), each baby's details, and the mother's discharge or referral.
- **Every live birth** is automatically enrolled in the **Child welfare clinic**. Birth and death notifications print from the system.
- **Child welfare clinic:** give vaccines from the **KEPI schedule** (due and overdue doses are shown) and record **growth** (weight, height) at each visit.
- **HEI follow-up:** enrol HIV-exposed infants, record each PCR/antibody test and its result, and **close** the follow-up with the final outcome.

## Specialist services

- **Blood bank:** **receive** donated units into stock, record **blood group and cross-match** for requests, **issue** compatible units, and record any **transfusion reaction**. **Discard** expired units with a reason.
- **Mortuary:** **record the death** (which produces the death notification), **admit the body**, and **release** it to the family with the receiver's details.
- **Renal/dialysis** and **specialist clinics** (oncology, physiotherapy, dental, eye, ENT, psychiatry) record sessions and visits on the patient's record, in the same way as an outpatient consultation.
